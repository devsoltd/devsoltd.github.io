Tappy Planet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who we are

Tappy Planet ("the game") is made by Devso, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, email devsoapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device.
  • The game shows optional video ads (which you choose to watch for rewards) using Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (stars, friends, upgrades, stickers, worlds) and your settings (music, sounds, vibration) are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or using "Reset game" in the menu deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • device type, operating system and app version
  • IP address and approximate (not precise) location
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was watched)

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. You can read how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: when you first open the game, you are asked whether you consent to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Menu → Privacy in the game.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional. You can play the whole game without watching any.

Sharing stickers

If you choose to share a sticker, the game creates a picture and opens your phone's normal share menu. Where it goes is up to you; the game does not upload or keep a copy of it.

Ratings

The game may show Google Play's built-in rating window. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Tappy Planet is a general audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We do not run servers that store player data. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. As we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, but you are welcome to contact us at devsoapp@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
